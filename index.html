    Keep the app private and keep your records safe.

    App lock

    The lock asks for the PIN when you open the app and when you come back after a short break. It keeps casual snoopers out on this phone only. It is not encryption, and each phone has its own PIN. If you forget it, clear this site's data in your browser settings; your records stay in your Google Sheet or account.

    Backup and restore

    Save everything to one file you keep yourself. Restore adds it back, replacing anything with the same ID.
